Who you’ll be working with

Two singers, not an agency.

When you write to LA Studio Voices, one of us writes back. We’ve stood on the other side of the mic for years, and we build every session the way we’d want to be treated.

Founder · Contractor & vocalist

Amick Byram

Amick Byram is a 2× Grammy Award–nominated vocalist whose voice has been featured in hundreds of films and television series, including The Prince of Egypt (as the singing voice of Moses), La La Land, Star Wars, The Simpsons, and Family Guy — making him one of the most widely heard yet behind-the-scenes singers in the industry.

Amick on IMDb

Co-founder · Vocal director

Jonathan Byram

Jonathan Byram is a vocalist, composer, songwriter, and music director. He has sung with Adele, Björk, and Josh Groban, and for Michael Giacchino, John Debney, and Marc Shaiman. His singing credits include Hocus Pocus 2, Ted, Hairspray, High School Musical, The Blacklist, the Happy Honda Days commercial, and more.

What we do

Whatever the project, we’ll find its voice.

  • Film, TV & game scores

    Choirs, soloists, and specialty voices for scoring sessions. Union paperwork handled.

  • Live & touring

    Backing vocalists and ensembles for tours, broadcasts, and one-night-only events.

  • Vocal demos

    The right singer for your song, fast: pitch demos, workshops, reference vocals.

  • Records & sessions

    Group vocals, stacks, harmonies, and features for artists and producers.

  • Vocal direction

    We can run the room too — arranging, coaching, and leading the singers.

How it works

  1. Say hello

    Email us whatever you know so far. A rough idea and a date is plenty.

  2. We shape the sound

    We’ll talk it through, suggest the right voices, and send a clear, no-surprises quote.

  3. You get your voices

    We handle booking, contracts, and union paperwork, then show up ready to make it sing.
